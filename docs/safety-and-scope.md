# Safety and scope

CrediTwin-Aero is an educational research demonstrator for an inert, low-voltage, electrically heated article. It deliberately excludes energetic materials, combustion, internal ballistics, flight performance, guidance and weapon-effect modelling.

The software is not flight-qualified, safety-certified or validated for operational hardware. Synthetic results must not be presented as experimental evidence. Any physical build requires component documentation, electrical protection, independent temperature limiting and an appropriate laboratory risk assessment.
