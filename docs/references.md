# Research basis

1. Li, L., Aslam, S., Wileman, A. & Perinpanayagam, S. (2022). Digital Twin in Aerospace Industry: A Gentle Introduction. IEEE Access, 10, 9543-9562. DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3136458.
2. Xiao, F., Qian, F., Su, B. & Yu, Y. (2018). Digital Twin of Solid Rocket Motor, Problem and Challenge. 11th International Symposium on Computational Intelligence and Design. DOI: 10.1109/ISCID.2018.10103.

The source papers are not redistributed in this repository. The propulsion paper informs high-level digital-thread and model-integration architecture only; this project excludes motor-performance and energetic-material modelling.
